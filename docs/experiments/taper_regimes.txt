A variant that wins in the regime it was tuned for and loses elsewhere is fitted to that regime. 'better in' counts
regimes where the mean difference is above zero; it is not a test, and every regime uses the same seeds.

Read this with care: the fills are simulated (queue model, no market impact), the markets come from one synthetic
generator (regimes change its settings, not its kind), and 3 comparisons were made without adjusting p for that. A small p here says a variant beat the base on
these generated markets, not that it would make money anywhere. PnL = edge + inventory: a gain that sits in the inventory
column is the price drifting under a position, which is luck as much as skill. With n markets the smallest possible sign p
is 2/2^n (0.0313 here).
