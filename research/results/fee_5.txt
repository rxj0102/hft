Read this with care: the fills are simulated (queue model, no market impact), the markets come from one synthetic
generator, and 4 comparisons were made without adjusting p for that. A small p here says a variant beat the base on
these generated markets, not that it would make money anywhere. PnL = edge + inventory: a gain that sits in the inventory
column is the price drifting under a position, which is luck as much as skill. With n markets the smallest possible sign p
is 2/2^n (0.00781 here).
